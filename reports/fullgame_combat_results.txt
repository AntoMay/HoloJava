# HoloJava Combat Results

## Combat Input System
- Player-triggered attacks through centralized input mapping layer
- Held-key repeat supported according to cooldown
- Duplicate attack prevention from key polling and repeat events
- Separate attack, movement, confirm, skill, special attack, and pause inputs
- Combat input does not leak into menus, pause, or level-overlay screens
- Autonomous orbiting and passive weapons preserved

## Weapon Lifecycle
- Basic attacks with cooldown management
- Weapon categories: melee arcs, straight projectiles, orbiting weapons, boomerangs, AoE, homing, piercing, defensive barriers
- Cooldown, damage, collision, lifetime, and visuals consistent
- Bounded effect pools for particle effects
- Effects never apply damage independently of combat simulation
- Prevent unintended repeated damage and stale state after entity reuse

## Enemy AI
- Chaser behavior: pursues player contact damage
- Shooter behavior: fires projectiles on cooldown
- Charger behavior: fast movement toward player
- Bomber behavior: explodes after timer or contact
- Healer behavior: restores player or enemy HP
- Weighted pool selection, edge spawn, cap, and scaling implemented

## Damage Formula
- Weapon damage stat modified by player ATK percentage
- Critical hit probability and multiplier implemented
- Knockback applied based on weapon weight and speed
- Damage numbers displayed but not independently verified

## Collab Weapons
- Collab weapons fuse two weapon components at max level
- Golden Anvil/Hammer required for fusion
- One super collab per run limit
- Weapon prerequisites validated against catalog
